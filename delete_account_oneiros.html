Delete your Oneiros: AI Dream Art Journal account

This page explains how to delete your Oneiros: AI Dream Journal account and the data connected to it. The app is published by Velestra Apps.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Oneiros and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the danger zone and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm the two prompts.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email velestra.apps@proton.me from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Oneiros account". If you used Google or Apple sign-in, tell us which one. We will delete your account and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • All of your dreams, including the text you wrote.
  • All generated content: titles, symbols, interpretations and artwork images.
  • Your preferences and usage counters.

What may be kept

  • Copies in backups, which are removed within a reasonable period after deletion.
  • Subscription and purchase records held by Google Play, which we cannot delete and which Google keeps as required by law. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it in Google Play first.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.
